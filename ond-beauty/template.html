Beauty = products with business unit “beauty”. Brand, category and sub-category come from the product catalogue (item_d); “(none)” means the field is blank there. Target = daily GMV plan for the Beauty merch team (planning_dod_merch_brand_category_targets), set by country, brand and category; it follows the Category and Brand filters and is hidden when a Sub-category is selected. Targets are loaded up to 30 Sep 2026 so far. GMV is gross (incl. VAT, all item statuses including cancelled and returned). Units = gross items. ASP = GMV ÷ units. MKD % = markdown discount ÷ normal price (AED). PC1 % = PC1 ÷ gross revenue. Prov. PC1 % = provisional PC1 ÷ gross revenue. Overall covers all countries, KSA and UAE included. Ratios are calculated from summed values. Change vs LY/LLY is % for value metrics and percentage points (pp) for % metrics. Today is excluded because the day is still in progress.
