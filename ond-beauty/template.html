What each metric means and how it is calculated.

Data Notes & Methodology

Sources, scope and how the numbers are built. Data from 1 Oct; LY and LLY on like-for-like dates. Refreshed .

  • LY = the same metric on the LY LFL dates (stock-type metrics: the LY LFL day).
  • Contrib. vs LY = (row TY − row LY) ÷ total LY, in pp: the rows add up to the total’s % change vs LY, so the most negative rows drove the drop.
  • WoC: a fall vs LY / LLY shows green (fewer weeks of cover), a rise red.
  • Share and age-band cells are shaded: darker = higher %, relative to the highest row shown.
  • WoC (units) = live retail stock ÷ (retail units sold in the last 30 days ÷ 30 × 7);
  • WoC (COGS) = inventory value at cost ÷ (retail COGS of the last 30 days ÷ 30 × 7); units and COGS are business model retail only (sales_metrics cogs).
  • MTD performance: every metric for the selected month (or OND to date) by one cut, against target, LY and LLY on the chosen compare dates; orders, AOV and ITO are left out there because orders don’t add up across rows.
  • Krithi’s target = LY GMV on LFL dates × 1.25, for every row.
  • Month tgt = the full-month GMV target; Projection = MTD GMV ÷ MTD target × month target (the MTD achievement applied to the whole month, as on the summary tiles); Month ach % = projection ÷ month target. Shown for month periods, not for OND to date.
  • Health: stock / inventory value shares = the row’s part of the total; age bands = the row’s inventory value at cost by ageing band (namshi_analytics_custom.ageing_cost, sku_config_ageing_detailed, the oldest bucket since 1 Oct 2026; 0-90 = 0-89 days, … 720+), TY only; newness share = new assortment ÷ live assortment (TY: activated since 1 Jul 2026, LY: since 1 Jul 2025 up to the LY day).
  • Flow metrics add up the period; assortment, stock and inventory value show the last day of the period only (and the matching LY / LLY day).
  • The GMV target splits by country, category, brand, business model and brand type / group / POC (not by sub-category or season).
  • Orders by row are exact for country, category, sub-category, brand, business model and season without other filters; otherwise they are estimated from brand-level orders (≈), and rows can add up to more than the total because one order can hold several categories or brands.
  • Summary tiles: Platform = all categories on Namshi (same scope: Namshi marketplaces, GMV above zero, no samples & GWP) against the total GMV plan of all pods; it follows the region but not the product filters.
  • Beauty follows the region and filters.
  • Yesterday is the latest full day (DoD = vs the day before);
  • MTD runs from the 1st of that month; the month projection applies the MTD achievement to the full month — e.g. 90% of the MTD target projects 90% of the full-month target — and likewise against LY and LLY (LFL dates).
  • Inventory value (cost) = inventory_value_derived from retail_v1.stock_summary_history (Namshi retail partner 3001, live stock, eligible for Namshi); inventory value (retail) = stock × offer price on primary Namshi retail listings in psku_sku_mapped.
  • Both cover KSA and UAE and are daily levels.
  • Assortment & stock (psku_sku_mapped, KSA and UAE, live listings with stock above zero): live assortment = distinct live supplier SKUs (item_d supplier_sku); retail / MP assortment split it by business model;
  • CB assortment = supplier SKUs live in KSA from the UAE warehouse; new assortment = live supplier SKUs activated in that country (item_d activated_at_sa / activated_at_ae) between 1 Jul of that year and the day; live stock = stock_net on primary Namshi retail listings (partner 3001).
  • Overall assortment is KSA + UAE added together (a product live in both countries counts twice).
  • These are daily levels, so the 7-day and cumulative views and the “Since” chips show the daily average.
  • OOS GMV loss = estimated GMV lost to out-of-stock SKUs (looker.out_of_stock_new, oos_gmv_loss), available for KSA and UAE from 1 Jan 2025, so there is no LLY;
  • OOS loss / GMV divides it by KSA + UAE GMV.
  • Samples & GWP (item_d inventory type) are excluded from every metric.
  • Traffic comes from onsite measures (all platforms; app = iOS + Android): glance views (GVs), add to carts (ATC), search and non-search impressions.
  • ATC / GV = add to carts ÷ glance views.
  • UCR % = units sold ÷ glance views.
  • CTR % = glance views ÷ impressions (search + non-search, all platforms).
  • For traffic, very small brand rows on a day (under 5 GVs, no ATC, under 500 impressions) are grouped as “(other brands)” within their category and sub-category, so totals and category filters are exact.
  • Namshi marketplaces only (namshi, namshi_v2), lines with GMV above zero.
  • LFL date compares each day with its like-for-like day from the LFL mapping (for example Black Friday 2026 vs Black Friday 2025); where the mapping gives an LLY date in the wrong year (30 Sep and 31 Dec) the same calendar date is used.
  • Beauty = products with business unit “beauty”.
  • Brand, category, sub-category and business model come from the product catalogue (item_d);
  • “(none)” means the field is blank there.
  • Season comes from beauty_season_mapping (by sku_config), brand type from beauty_pestige_mastige_mapping, brand group from brand_group_beauty and brand POC from brand_poc_beauty; missing values are “unassigned”.
  • Traffic is kept either by brand or by business model / season, so with a brand filter (brand, type, group or POC) the business model and season filters are not applied to traffic, and traffic cards show “≈”.
  • The GMV target follows the category, brand (including brand type, group and POC) and business model filters, and is hidden when a sub-category or season is selected.
  • Targets are set by brand, so each brand counts under the business model that holds most of its GMV (Retail or MP).
  • Target = daily GMV plan for the Beauty pod (planning_dod_merch_brand_category_targets, pod cp_beauty), set by country, brand and category;
  • Targets are loaded up to 31 Oct 2026 so far.
  • GMV includes VAT and all item statuses (including cancelled and returned).
  • Units = gross items.
  • ASP = GMV ÷ units.
  • Orders = distinct order numbers holding Beauty items in scope.
  • AOV = Beauty GMV ÷ orders.
  • ITO = units ÷ orders.
  • Orders are exact for all Beauty and for one selected category, sub-category or brand; with several values or brand + category selected they are marked “≈” because an order holding more than one of them counts once per value.
  • MKD % = markdown discount ÷ normal price (AED).
  • PC1 % = PC1 ÷ gross revenue.
  • Prov.
  • PC1 % = provisional PC1 ÷ gross revenue.
  • Overall covers all countries, KSA and UAE included.
  • Ratios are calculated from summed values.
  • Change vs LY/LLY is % for value metrics and percentage points (pp) for % metrics.
  • Today is excluded because the day is still in progress.
