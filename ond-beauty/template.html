Daily table

MTD performance: every metric for the selected month (or OND to date) by one cut, against target, LY and LLY on the chosen compare dates. Flow metrics add up the period; assortment, stock and inventory value show the last day of the period only (and the matching LY / LLY day). The GMV target splits by country, category, brand, business model and brand type / group / POC (not by sub-category or season). Orders by row are exact for country, category, sub-category, brand, business model and season without other filters; otherwise they are estimated from brand-level orders (≈), and rows can add up to more than the total because one order can hold several categories or brands. Summary tiles: Platform = all categories on Namshi (same scope: Namshi marketplaces, GMV above zero, no samples & GWP) against the total GMV plan of all pods; it follows the region but not the product filters. Beauty follows the region and filters. Yesterday is the latest full day (DoD = vs the day before); MTD runs from the 1st of that month; the month projection applies the MTD achievement to the full month — e.g. 90% of the MTD target projects 90% of the full-month target — and likewise against LY and LLY (LFL dates). Inventory value (cost) = inventory_value_derived from retail_v1.stock_summary_history (Namshi retail partner 3001, live stock, eligible for Namshi); inventory value (retail) = stock × offer price on primary Namshi retail listings in psku_sku_mapped. Both cover KSA and UAE and are daily levels. Assortment & stock (psku_sku_mapped, KSA and UAE, live listings with stock above zero): live assortment = distinct live sku_configs; retail / MP assortment split it by business model; CB assortment = configs live in KSA from the UAE warehouse; live stock = stock_net on primary Namshi retail listings (partner 3001). Overall assortment is KSA + UAE added together (a product live in both countries counts twice). These are daily levels, so the 7-day and cumulative views and the “Since” chips show the daily average. OOS GMV loss = estimated GMV lost to out-of-stock SKUs (looker.out_of_stock_new, oos_gmv_loss), available for KSA and UAE from 1 Jan 2025, so there is no LLY; OOS loss / GMV divides it by KSA + UAE GMV. Samples & GWP (item_d inventory type) are excluded from every metric. Traffic comes from onsite measures (all platforms; app = iOS + Android): glance views (GVs), add to carts (ATC), search and non-search impressions. ATC / GV = add to carts ÷ glance views. UCR % = units sold ÷ glance views. CTR % = glance views ÷ impressions (search + non-search, all platforms). For traffic, very small brand rows on a day (under 5 GVs, no ATC, under 500 impressions) are grouped as “(other brands)” within their category and sub-category, so totals and category filters are exact. Namshi marketplaces only (namshi, namshi_v2), lines with GMV above zero. LFL date compares each day with its like-for-like day from the LFL mapping (for example Black Friday 2026 vs Black Friday 2025); where the mapping gives an LLY date in the wrong year (30 Sep and 31 Dec) the same calendar date is used. Beauty = products with business unit “beauty”. Brand, category, sub-category and business model come from the product catalogue (item_d); “(none)” means the field is blank there. Season comes from beauty_season_mapping (by sku_config), brand type from beauty_pestige_mastige_mapping, brand group from brand_group_beauty and brand POC from brand_poc_beauty; missing values are “unassigned”. Traffic is kept either by brand or by business model / season, so with a brand filter (brand, type, group or POC) the business model and season filters are not applied to traffic, and traffic cards show “≈”. The GMV target follows the category, brand (including brand type, group and POC) and business model filters, and is hidden when a sub-category or season is selected. Targets are set by brand, so each brand counts under the business model that holds most of its GMV (Retail or MP). Target = daily GMV plan for the Beauty pod (planning_dod_merch_brand_category_targets, pod cp_beauty), set by country, brand and category; Targets are loaded up to 31 Oct 2026 so far. GMV includes VAT and all item statuses (including cancelled and returned). Units = gross items. ASP = GMV ÷ units. Orders = distinct order numbers holding Beauty items in scope. AOV = Beauty GMV ÷ orders. ITO = units ÷ orders. Orders are exact for all Beauty and for one selected category, sub-category or brand; with several values or brand + category selected they are marked “≈” because an order holding more than one of them counts once per value. MKD % = markdown discount ÷ normal price (AED). PC1 % = PC1 ÷ gross revenue. Prov. PC1 % = provisional PC1 ÷ gross revenue. Overall covers all countries, KSA and UAE included. Ratios are calculated from summed values. Change vs LY/LLY is % for value metrics and percentage points (pp) for % metrics. Today is excluded because the day is still in progress.
